# Glimmer

Arcade survival: you're a wisp in a dark forest. Your light is your health, your vision, and your weapon. Survive until dawn.

**Play:** https://winecoolermike.github.io/glimmer/

Move: WASD/arrows or hold mouse · Flare: Space/Shift/right-click · Upgrades: 1/2/3 · Esc pause · M sound · K shake · R restart · Touch supported.

Docs: [Design doc](docs/GDD.md) · [Concepts](docs/concepts.md). Hobby prototype, vanilla HTML5/JS.
